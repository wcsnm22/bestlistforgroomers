About

Groomers Choice is a small static site that answers pet grooming tool questions - clipper, shampoo, brush, dryer and nail-care prices, shipping thresholds, stock labels and warranties - for six brands (Andis, Wahl, TropiClean, Chris Christensen, XPOWER and Nature's Miracle), with facts taken only from each brand's own official website and official product, help or policy pages.

How it works:

  • Every fact on this site carries the official source page URL it was read from and the date it was checked.
  • Nothing is estimated, rounded, or filled in from memory. If a figure is not published on the brand's official site, it is not repeated here.
  • Promotions are reported only when the brand's own site publishes them. Where no official promo page exists, the page says so.
  • We are not affiliated with, endorsed by, or compensated by any brand listed on this site. The manufacturer's own product page and specifications always govern.

The site is open source: https://github.com/wcsnm22/bestlistforgroomers. Facts were last checked 2026-09-30.
